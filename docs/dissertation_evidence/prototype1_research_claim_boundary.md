# Prototype 1 Research Claim Boundary

## Main Claim Supported by Prototype 1

"Prototype 1 supports the claim that local model-to-action generation is feasible in the project context."

## Evidence Type

The evidence is feasibility evidence, not final evaluation evidence. It supports the existence of an early local model-to-action pathway, but it does not establish that the generated output is safe, semantically correct, execution-ready or representative across a benchmark.

## Claim Strength

Moderate feasibility claim.

The claim is strong enough to justify the next prototype, but not strong enough to support final claims about safety, validity, deployment or comparative performance. Prototype 1 should therefore be used as the dissertation's foundation layer, not as its final evidence layer.

## Research Question Answered

Can a local model produce action-like outputs relevant to task planning?

Answer: Yes, at feasibility level.

## Research Questions Not Yet Answered

- Can the output be deterministically validated?
- Can unsafe outputs be rejected before execution?
- How reliable is the model across a fixed benchmark?
- How does local inference compare with cloud inference?
- What local resource pressure does Foundry Local impose?
- How do model-family and quantisation choices affect deployment?

## Relationship to Final Dissertation Claim

Prototype 1 is one part of the final dissertation evidence chain. It does not independently prove the final contribution, but it establishes the feasibility premise needed for the zero-trust framework. Later prototypes provide the validation, benchmarking, execution-grounding, resource and comparison evidence needed for the final dissertation argument.

## Examiner-Safe Wording

- "Prototype 1 demonstrated initial local model-to-action feasibility."
- "Prototype 1 motivated the need for deterministic validation."
- "Prototype 1 did not establish execution safety."
- "Prototype 1 did not provide benchmark-level generalisation."

## Wording to Avoid

- "Prototype 1 proved the model was safe."
- "Prototype 1 produced executable robot commands."
- "Prototype 1 showed local inference was better than cloud."
- "Prototype 1 completed the Microsoft brief."
- "Prototype 1 demonstrated production readiness."
